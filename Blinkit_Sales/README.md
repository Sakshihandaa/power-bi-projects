# Blinkit Grocery Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing grocery retail performance, outlet expansion trends, product category demand, and customer ratings for Blinkit.

---

## 📊 Key KPIs
* **Total Sales:** $1.20M
* **No. of Items:** 8,523
* **Average Sales:** $141
* **Average Rating:** 3.92

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features
* **Outlet Establishment:** Area chart tracking sales growth by establishment year, peaking at $205K in 2018.
* **Product & Fat Content Analysis:** Donut and bar charts comparing Low Fat vs. Regular items and top categories (Fruits & Vegetables, Snack Foods).
* **Outlet Size & Location:** Donut and funnel charts breaking down revenue across Medium ($508K), Small ($445K), and High ($249K) outlets, as well as Tier 1–3 locations.
* **Outlet Type Matrix:** Table comparing Total Sales, Item Count, Avg Sales, Avg Rating, and Item Visibility across Supermarket Types and Grocery Stores.
* **Dynamic Slicers & Field Parameters:** Interactive filtering by Outlet Location, Size, Item Type, plus metric toggles (`Avg_Rating`, `Avg_Sales`, `No_Of_Items`, `Total Sales`) and a reset button.
